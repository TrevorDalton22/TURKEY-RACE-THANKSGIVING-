# TURKEY RACE! [THANKSGIVING]

Roblox game concept — shareable notes

## The pitch

A fast, chaotic Thanksgiving-themed multiplayer racing game where players ARE turkeys trying to avoid becoming Thanksgiving dinner. Race through oversized holiday environments, grab silly food-themed power-ups, and outrun a giant angry chef.

## Core hook

**"DON'T GET COOKED!"**

Players control waddling, wing-flapping turkeys rather than cars. The racing should feel funny and slightly chaotic, but still reward skill.

## Main gameplay loop

1. Join a lobby and choose your turkey.
2. Enter a quick race with 8–12 players (target: 90 seconds–2 minutes).
3. Sprint, drift, jump, and flap across a Thanksgiving obstacle course.
4. Collect power-ups to boost yourself or disrupt opponents.
5. Survive the chef, finish the race, and earn Feathers and Wins.
6. Spend Feathers on turkey skins, trails, animations, and other cosmetics; queue again.

## Signature feature: the chef chase

Near the end of each race, an enormous angry chef appears with a spatula or frying pan. He chases the pack, especially the turkeys at the back. Getting hit sends a turkey flying and costs precious time. This creates funny, shareable moments.

## Optional mode: Don't Get Cooked (elimination)

At each checkpoint or short round, the slowest turkey is eliminated and comically sent to the oven. The final three race for the win. This could be the flagship mode or a later update; test it against traditional racing.

## Map ideas

- **Thanksgiving Dinner Dash:** Race across a giant dinner table; dodge forks, food, and slippery gravy.
- **Pumpkin Patch Panic:** Dash through corn mazes, hay bales, and rolling pumpkins.
- **Escape the Oven:** Jump across kitchen counters and stovetops while escaping the chef.

## Power-ups

- **Pie Launcher:** Throw a pie at another racer.
- **Drumstick Dash:** Short speed boost.
- **Butter Slide:** Leave a slippery hazard behind.
- **Gobble Bomb:** Knock nearby turkeys away.
- **Mashed Potato Shield:** Block attacks.
- **Corn Tornado:** Briefly spin opponents around.

## Collectibles / customization

- **Turkey skins:** Cool Turkey, King Turkey, Lava Turkey, Ghost Turkey, Chef Turkey, Golden Turkey.
- **Other unlocks:** feather trails, funny gobble sounds, victory animations, emotes, and name tags.

## Progression

- Earn Feathers for playing, finishing, winning, and completing daily challenges.
- Track Wins and offer a global leaderboard.
- Add daily rotating challenges and seasonal rewards for repeat play.
- Keep speed and competitive performance mostly skill-based.

## First playable version (MVP)

- 1 simple Thanksgiving-themed lobby
- 1 polished race mode with 8 players
- 1 well-designed race map to start; expand to 3
- Basic waddling, sprinting, jumping, flapping, and drifting
- 3 power-ups initially; expand to 6
- Working race timer, checkpoints, finish/results screen, and rewards
- 4 turkey skins initially; expand to 8
- Chef chase as the standout feature
- Wins leaderboard and a basic shop

## Launch / marketing

- **Working title:** "TURKEY RACE! [THANKSGIVING]"
- **Alternative title:** "Turkey Turbo!"
- **Thumbnail:** Several panicked turkeys racing toward the camera with a giant chef chasing them. Big readable text: "DON'T GET COOKED!"
- **Trailers/short clips:** Show ridiculous chef knockbacks, close finishes, pie attacks, and funny turkey movement.
- **Best timing:** Launch or update around the Thanksgiving season, while keeping the game playable after the holiday.

## Why it could stand out

- Turkeys as the playable racers are a funny visual hook.
- Holiday-themed oversized tracks give the game a distinct identity.
- Racing + chef chase + optional elimination mode creates high-stakes moments.
- Short matches are easy to replay and share.

## Originality note

The combination seems distinctive, but individual racing, power-up, and elimination mechanics already exist in other games. Treat originality as a working hypothesis, not a guarantee; check Roblox search again before launch.

## Big decision for the team

Choose a primary mode:

- **A) Mario Kart-style racing:** Complete laps, use power-ups, and finish first.
- **B) Elimination racing:** The slowest turkeys get "cooked" until one remains.

**Recommendation:** Prototype both with the same movement and map, then playtest which is more fun.
